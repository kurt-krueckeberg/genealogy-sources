= Indiana Historic Cemeteries

Within the link:https://www.in.gov/dnr[Indiana Department of Natural Resources (DNR)],
its link:https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/[Division of Historic Preservation & Archaeology] maintains a
link:https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/county-survey-program/shaard-database[Cemetery & Burial Ground
Registry] for all Indiana cemeteries and burial grounds, not just “historic” cemeteries. 

If you go to link:https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/[Indiana Department of Natural Resources] -> 
link:https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/cemeteries[Cemeteries], you will see links to:

* The link:https://secure.in.gov/apps/dnr/shaard/welcome.html[Indiana State Historic Architectural and
  Archaeological Research Database (SHAARD)], and
* The link:https://gisdata.in.gov/portal/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=5f2c93ba15a6423b906fec4a2e986b85[Indiana Historic
Buildings, Bridges, and Cemeteries (IHBBC) Map].

== How To SHAARD and IHBBC

=== SHAARD Steps

[arabic]
. You can access the link:https://secure.in.gov/apps/dnr/shaard/welcome.html[database] as a guest ("Enter SHAARD as a guest).  
. Choose *Survey Type* of "Cemetery Register"
. Specify *County* of "Allen" and *Legal Townshop* of "Marion".
. Click *Search* and wait because the site is slow.
. Look for the *Historic Name* of "Saint John's Evangelical Lutheran" with *Address" of "Wayne Trace".
