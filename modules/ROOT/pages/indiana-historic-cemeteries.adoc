= Indiana Historic Cemeteries

The *Indiana Department of Natural Resources (DNR)'s* *Division of Historic
Preservation & Archaeology (DHPA)* maintains a registry of cemeteries and
burial grounds that includes known sites across the state, and this *does
encompass historic cemeteries, including those that _formerly existed_* if their
locations have been documented.
(https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/cemeteries/?utm_source=chatgpt.com[Government
of Indiana])

Here are the key points you should know:

== 1. _Cemetery & Burial Ground Registry_

* The DHPA maintains a *Cemetery & Burial Ground Registry* covering
*cemeteries and burial grounds throughout Indiana*.
* This registry *is not limited to actively used cemeteries* — it
includes *historic burial sites* whose locations and attributes have
been documented, even if they are no longer in use or the headstones
have deteriorated.
(https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/cemeteries/?utm_source=chatgpt.com[Government
of India])

== 2. *Inclusion of Historic and Former Cemeteries*

* The registry is intended to document *all known cemeteries and burial
grounds in each county*, regardless of size, condition, or whether they
are currently maintained.
* This means *some cemeteries that existed in the late 19th and early
20th centuries — including those no longer visible or in active use —
can be recorded in the state database* _if they were identified and
surveyed_.
(https://law.justia.com/codes/indiana/title-14/article-21/chapter-1/section-14-21-1-13-5/?utm_source=chatgpt.com[Justia
Law])

== 3. *How To Access the Data*

* Cemetery information, including historic and former cemetery entries,
is available through the *Indiana State Historic Architectural and
Archaeological Research Database (SHAARD)*.
(https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/county-survey-program/shaard-database/?utm_source=chatgpt.com[Government
of India])
* Within SHAARD, these records may appear as individual entries with
location information, descriptive data, and sometimes survey forms and
general condition notes.

== 4. *Mapping Tools*

* Some cemetery locations (including historic ones) are shown on the
*Indiana Historic Buildings, Bridges, and Cemeteries (IHBBC) Map*, a GIS
viewer linked to SHAARD data.
(https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/county-survey-program/shaard-database/?utm_source=chatgpt.com[Government
of India])
* Note that *not all historic or former cemeteries are mapped* if
precise geographic coordinates are unknown; some may have a registry
entry without a point on the map.
(https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/cemeteries/?utm_source=chatgpt.com[Government
of India])

== 5. *Supplemental Sources*

In addition to the DNR/DHPA registry, you may find information about
historic and former cemeteries from other Indiana sources that compile
cemetery data, such as:

* *Indiana Cemetery Locator File* at the Indiana State Library — a
searchable list of cemetery names and locations.
(https://www.statelib.lib.in.us/cemetery/cemetery_search.asp?utm_source=chatgpt.com[Indiana
State Library])
* *Third-party cemetery registries* that collate verified or unverified
historic cemetery entries statewide.
(https://cemeteryregistry.us/find-a-cemetery/indiana-cemeteries?utm_source=chatgpt.com[CemeteryRegistry.US])
* *Local historical societies and genealogy projects* that identify
cemeteries no longer visible on the landscape.

in the 19th and early 20th centuries, even if they are no longer extant
on the surface or in active use.* These are accessible through SHAARD
and, for mapped sites, through the IHBBC GIS viewer.
