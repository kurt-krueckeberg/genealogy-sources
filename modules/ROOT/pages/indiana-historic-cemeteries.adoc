= Indiana Historic Cemeteries

Within the link:https://www.in.gov/dnr[Indiana Department of Natural Resources (DNR)],
the link:https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/[Division of Historic Preservation & Archaeology] maintains a
*Cemetery & Burial Ground Registry* for all Indiana cemeteries and burial grounds, not just “historic” cemeteries. 

Navigate to link:https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/[Indiana Department of Natural Resources] -> 
link:https://www.in.gov/dnr/historic-preservation/cemeteries[Cemeteries]. You will find a link to

* The *Indiana State Historic Architectural and Archaeological Research Database (SHAARD)*, and
* The *Indiana Historic Buildings, Bridges, and Cemeteries (IHBBC) Map*.
