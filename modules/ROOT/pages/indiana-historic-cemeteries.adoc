= Indiana Historic Cemeteries

The *Division of Historic Preservation & Archaeology (DHPA)* of the *Indiana Department of Natural Resources (DNR)*
maintains a Cemetery & Burial Ground Registry for all Indiana cemeteries and burial grounds, not just “historic”
cemeteries.
