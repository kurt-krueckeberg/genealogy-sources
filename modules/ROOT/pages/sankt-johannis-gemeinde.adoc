= Peppler and Sankt Johannis Gemeinde, Fort Wayne, Indiana Resources

== Sankt Johannis Gemeinde (St. John Parish) on Wayne Trace near I-469

The Pepplers were members of the Sankt Johannis Gemeinde, just north of Emmanuel
(Soest), on the esat side of Wayne Trace just north day of I-469. They
were a splinter congregation of Emmanuel according it
http://www.emmanuelsoest.org/our-history/[history].
