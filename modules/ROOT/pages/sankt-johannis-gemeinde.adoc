== Sankt Johannis Gemeinde (St. John Parish) on Wayne Trace near I-469

is on the Immanuel-Soest website.

== Genealogy Center Volumes for St. John's/Sankt Johannis

*Note:* There is a St. John's Lutheran church on Washington Street. This is a different church.

The Genealogy Center's link:https://www.genealogycenter.info/results_allenstjohannis.php[online burial records] for
St. Johannis Cemetery] , Allen County, Indiana, compiled by Jean Hite Patee from the burial records in its
Kirchenbuch. 

*NOTE:* The link above mentions that the records of Sankt Johannis/St. John were later deposited with Grace
Lutheran that itself later disbanded.
